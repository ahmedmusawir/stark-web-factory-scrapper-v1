### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS
All parameters listed below represent the structure of the data payload received when an invoice is deleted. The documentation does not explicitly distinguish between required and optional fields within this payload; all fields shown in the schema are expected to be part of the event data.

*   **_id** (string): The unique identifier of the deleted invoice.
*   **status** (string): The status of the invoice, typically "delete" for this event.
*   **liveMode** (boolean): Indicates if the event occurred in live mode (true) or test mode (false).
*   **amountPaid** (number): The amount paid on the invoice before deletion.
*   **altId** (string): An alternative identifier for the invoice, possibly related to its context (e.g., location ID).
*   **altType** (string): The type associated with the `altId` (e.g., "location").
*   **name** (string): The name of the invoice.
*   **businessDetails** (object): Details of the business associated with the invoice.
    *   **name** (string): Name of the business.
    *   **address** (string): Address of the business.
    *   **phoneNo** (string): Phone number of the business.
    *   **website** (string): Website of the business.
    *   **logoUrl** (string): URL to the business logo.
    *   **customValues** (array of string): Custom values associated with the business.
*   **invoiceNumber** (string): The number assigned to the invoice.
*   **currency** (string): The currency of the invoice (e.g., "USD").
*   **contactDetails** (object): Details of the contact associated with the invoice.
    *   **id** (string): The unique identifier of the contact.
    *   **phoneNo** (string): Phone number of the contact.
    *   **email** (string): Email of the contact.
    *   **customFields** (array of string): Custom fields for the contact.
    *   **name** (string): Name of the contact.
    *   **address** (object): Address details of the contact.
        *   **countryCode** (string): Country code (e.g., "US").
        *   **addressLine1** (string): First line of the address.
        *   **addressLine2** (string): Second line of the address.
        *   **city** (string): City.
        *   **state** (string): State.
        *   **postalCode** (string): Postal code.
    *   **additionalEmails** (array of objects): Additional email addresses for the contact.
        *   **email** (string): An additional email address.
    *   **companyName** (string): Company name of the contact.
*   **issueDate** (string): The date the invoice was issued (ISO 8601 format).
*   **dueDate** (string): The date the invoice was due (ISO 8601 format).
*   **discount** (object): Details of any discount applied to the invoice.
    *   **type** (string): Type of discount (e.g., "percentage").
    *   **value** (number): Value of the discount.
*   **invoiceItems** (array of objects): List of items included in the invoice.
    *   **taxes** (array): Array of tax details (structure not detailed but shown as empty in example).
    *   **_id** (string): Unique identifier for the invoice item.
    *   **productId** (string): The ID of the product.
    *   **priceId** (string): The ID of the price.
    *   **currency** (string): Currency of the item.
    *   **name** (string): Name of the item.
    *   **qty** (number): Quantity of the item.
    *   **amount** (number): Amount for the item.
*   **total** (number): The grand total amount of the invoice.
*   **title** (string): The title of the invoice.
*   **amountDue** (number): The remaining amount due on the invoice.
*   **createdAt** (string): Timestamp when the invoice was created (ISO 8601 format).
*   **updatedAt** (string): Timestamp when the invoice was last updated (ISO 8601 format).
*   **totalSummary** (object): Summary of total amounts.
    *   **subTotal** (number): Subtotal before discounts.
    *   **discount** (number): Total discount amount.
