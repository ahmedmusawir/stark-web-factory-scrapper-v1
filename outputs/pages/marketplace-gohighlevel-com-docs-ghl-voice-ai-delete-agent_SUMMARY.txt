*   **Scope(s):** `voice-ai-agents.write`

*   **401 Unauthorized:**
    *   `application/json`
    *   Schema: `{"statusCode": number, "message": string, "error": string}`
    *   Example: `{"statusCode":401,"message":"Invalid token: access token is invalid","error":"Unauthorized"}`
*   **422 Unprocessable Entity:**
    *   `application/json`

This endpoint allows users to delete a voice AI agent and all its associated configurations.
