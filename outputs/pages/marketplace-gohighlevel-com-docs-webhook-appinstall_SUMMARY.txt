### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS
These parameters are consistently present in all provided examples for the webhook payload.
-   **type** (string): The type of event. Value is always "INSTALL".
-   **appId** (string): The unique identifier of the installed app.
-   **companyId** (string): The unique identifier of the company (agency or sub-account) where the app is installed.
    *Note: The documentation states "The User ID and Company ID may be available when a new token is generated. In case of app installation via future locations, you may not get these fields." However, `companyId` is present in all provided examples.*
-   **planId** (string): The unique identifier of the plan associated with the app installation.
-   **trial** (object): An object containing details about the trial period for the app.
    -   **onTrial** (boolean): Indicates if the app is currently on a trial.
    -   **trialDuration** (number): The duration of the trial in days.
    -   **trialStartDate** (ISO 8601 Date string): The start date and time of the trial.

### 7. OPTIONAL PARAMETERS
These parameters are conditionally present in the webhook payload, depending on whether the app is installed at the location level or agency level, and other specific conditions.
-   **locationId** (string): The unique identifier of the sub-account (formerly location) where the app is installed.
    *Present for Location Level App Installs, absent for Agency Level App Installs.*
-   **userId** (string): The unique identifier of the user who performed the app installation.
    *Present for Location Level App Installs, absent for Agency Level App Installs.*
-   **isWhitelabelCompany** (boolean): Indicates whether the company where the app is installed is a whitelabel company.
    *Present for Location Level App Installs.*
-   **whitelabelDetails** (object): An object containing details about the company's whitelabel setup.
    *Present for Location Level App Installs if `isWhitelabelCompany` is true. Will be an empty object (`{}`) if `isWhitelabelCompany` is false.*
    -   **domain** (string): The whitelabel domain of the company.
    -   **logoUrl** (string): The URL of the whitelabel logo.
-   **companyName** (string): The name of the company where the app is installed.
    *Present for Location Level App Installs.*
