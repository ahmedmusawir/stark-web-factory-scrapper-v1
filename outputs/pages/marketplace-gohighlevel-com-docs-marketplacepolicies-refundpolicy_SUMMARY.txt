*   **Refund Windows/Conditions:**
    *   **Subscription-based Apps:** Prorated refund within 3 days of initial purchase (unless developer specifies otherwise). No refunds after free trial ends for the initial billing cycle. Renewals are generally non-refundable.
    *   **One-time fee/Lifetime license Apps:** No refunds unless the product is defective or non-functional. Must contact developer support within 3 days of purchase with proof.
    *   **Usage-based Apps:** Refunds only for incorrect billing or errors (contact support within 3 days of charge). No refunds for legitimate and accurately tracked usage.
    *   **Apps That Process Payments Outside HighLevel:** HighLevel does not issue refunds; users must contact the developer directly.
