### WEBHOOK PAYLOAD FIELDS:
*   `type` (string): The type of the webhook event. For this event, it is "OpportunityUpdate".
*   `locationId` (string): The unique identifier of the location (sub-account) where the opportunity was updated.
*   `id` (string): The unique identifier of the opportunity that was updated.
*   `assignedTo` (string): The unique identifier of the user to whom the opportunity is assigned.
*   `contactId` (string): The unique identifier of the contact associated with the opportunity.
*   `monetaryValue` (number): The monetary value of the opportunity.
*   `name` (string): The name of the opportunity.
*   `pipelineId` (string): The unique identifier of the pipeline to which the opportunity belongs.
*   `pipelineStageId` (string): The unique identifier of the stage within the pipeline where the opportunity currently resides. (Note: This field appears twice in the provided schema, likely a redundancy in the documentation schema definition.)
*   `source` (string): The source from which the opportunity originated.
*   `status` (string): The current status of the opportunity (e.g., "open").
*   `dateAdded` (string): The date and time when the opportunity was initially added, in ISO 8601 format (e.g., "2021-11-26T12:41:02.193Z").
