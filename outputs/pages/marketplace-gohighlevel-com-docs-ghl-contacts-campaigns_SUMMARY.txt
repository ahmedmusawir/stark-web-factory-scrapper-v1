This overview page lists the following specific API operations, with links to their dedicated documentation:

*   **Add Contact to Campaign**
    *   **Purpose:** To enroll a specified contact into a particular campaign.
    *   **Documentation Link:** [https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/contacts/add-contact-to-campaign](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/contacts/add-contact-to-campaign)

*   **Remove Contact From Campaign**
    *   **Purpose:** To unenroll a specified contact from a particular campaign.
    *   **Documentation Link:** [https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/contacts/remove-contact-from-campaign](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/contacts/remove-contact-from-campaign)

*   **Remove Contact From Every Campaign**
    *   **Purpose:** To unenroll a specified contact from all campaigns they are currently part of.
    *   **Documentation Link:** [https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/contacts/remove-contact-from-every-campaign](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/contacts/remove-contact-from-every-campaign)
