### 1. ENDPOINT NAME:
Update schedule

### 2. CATEGORY:
Invoice Schedule

### 3. HTTP METHOD:
PUT

### 4. ENDPOINT URL:
`/invoices/schedule/:scheduleId`

### 5. FULL URL:
`https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/invoices/schedule/:scheduleId`
