### 7. OPTIONAL PARAMETERS (in the webhook payload):
These parameters may be present or absent, or have empty values, depending on the user type (sub-account vs. agency) or specific configuration.

*   **locationId** (string): The unique identifier of the sub-account (location) if the user belongs to a sub-account. (Mutually exclusive with `companyId`).
*   **companyId** (string): The unique identifier of the agency if the user belongs to an agency. (Mutually exclusive with `locationId`).
*   **scopes** (array of string): An array of authorization scopes associated with the user. Can be an empty array.
*   **locations** (array of string): For agency users, an array of location IDs they have access to. Can be an empty array.

### 10. PURPOSE:
This webhook is called by the HighLevel platform to notify subscribers whenever a user is deleted from a sub-account or agency.
