### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS (Payload Fields):
*   **id** (string): Unique identifier for the created association.
*   **firstObjectKey** (string): Key representing the first object in the association.
*   **firstRecordId** (string): Identifier of the first object’s specific record.
*   **secondObjectKey** (string): Key representing the second object in the association.
*   **secondRecordId** (string): Identifier of the second object’s specific record.
*   **associationId** (string): Unique identifier for the association that was created.
*   **locationId** (string): Identifies the location associated with the created association.
