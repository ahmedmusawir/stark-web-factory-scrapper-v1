### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS (Webhook Payload Fields):
These are the fields included in the JSON payload sent by the webhook.
*   **`type`** (string): Indicates the type of record created.
*   **`locationId`** (string): Identifies the location associated with the created record.
*   **`owners`** (array of strings): Represents the unique identifiers of users who own the record.
*   **`followers`** (array of strings): List of users who are following the record for updates.
*   **`properties`** (array of objects): Contains key-value pairs representing additional details about the record.
    *   **`key`** (string): The property name.
    *   **`valueString`** (string): The corresponding value as a string.
*   **`id`** (string): Unique identifier for the created record.
*   **`timestamp`** (string, format: `date-time`): Represents the date and time when the record was created.
