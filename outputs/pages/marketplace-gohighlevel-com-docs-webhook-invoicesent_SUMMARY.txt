Here's a structured summary of the API documentation for the InvoiceSent webhook:

### 1. ENDPOINT NAME
InvoiceSent

### 2. CATEGORY
Webhook (specifically, Invoice related events)

### 3. HTTP METHOD
This is a webhook event, not a callable API endpoint. HighLevel sends an HTTP POST request to a registered URL when an invoice is sent.

### 4. ENDPOINT URL
Not applicable. This is a webhook event that is *sent by* HighLevel to a URL configured by the user.

### 5. FULL URL
Not applicable. (Documentation URL: `https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/webhook/InvoiceSent/index.html`)

### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS
These parameters represent the payload sent by the webhook event. All fields listed in the schema are considered part of the payload for this event.

*   **_id** (string): Unique identifier for the invoice.
*   **status** (string): Current status of the invoice (e.g., "sent").
*   **liveMode** (boolean): Indicates if the event occurred in live mode.
*   **amountPaid** (number): The amount that has been paid for the invoice.
*   **altId** (string): Alternate identifier for the invoice, possibly related to the `altType`.
*   **altType** (string): Type of the alternate ID (e.g., "location").
*   **name** (string): Name of the invoice (e.g., "New Invoice").
*   **businessDetails** (object): Details of the business associated with the invoice.
    *   **name** (string): Name of the business.
    *   **address** (string): Business address.
    *   **phoneNo** (string): Business phone number.
    *   **website** (string): Business website URL.
    *   **logoUrl** (string): URL to the business logo.
    *   **customValues** (array of string): Custom values associated with the business details.
*   **invoiceNumber** (string): The invoice number.
*   **currency** (string): The currency of the invoice (e.g., "USD").
*   **contactDetails** (object): Details of the contact associated with the invoice.
    *   **id** (string): Unique identifier for the contact.
    *   **phoneNo** (string): Contact's phone number.
    *   **email** (string): Contact's email address.
    *   **customFields** (array of string): Custom fields associated with the contact.
    *   **name** (string): Contact's name.
    *   **address** (object): Contact's address details.
        *   **countryCode** (string): Two-letter country code.
        *   **addressLine1** (string): First line of the address.
        *   **addressLine2** (string): Second line of the address.
        *   **city** (string): City.
        *   **state** (string): State or province.
        *   **postalCode** (string): Postal or ZIP code.
    *   **additionalEmails** (array of object): Additional email addresses for the contact.
        *   **email** (string): An additional email address.
    *   **companyName** (string): Name of the contact's company.
*   **issueDate** (string): Date when the invoice was issued (ISO 8601 format).
*   **dueDate** (string): Date when the invoice is due (ISO 8601 format).
*   **discount** (object): Discount applied to the invoice.
    *   **type** (string): Type of discount (e.g., "percentage").
    *   **value** (number): Value of the discount.
*   **invoiceItems** (array of object): List of items included in the invoice.
    *   **taxes** (array): Array of taxes applied to the item (structure not detailed).
    *   **_id** (string): Unique identifier for the invoice item.
    *   **productId** (string): Identifier for the product associated with the item.
    *   **priceId** (string): Identifier for the price associated with the item.
    *   **currency** (string): Currency of the item.
    *   **name** (string): Name of the invoice item.
    *   **qty** (number): Quantity of the item.
    *   **amount** (number): Amount per item.
*   **total** (number): Total amount of the invoice before discounts or taxes.
*   **title** (string): Title of the invoice (e.g., "INVOICE").
*   **amountDue** (number): Remaining amount due on the invoice.
*   **createdAt** (string): Timestamp when the invoice was created (ISO 8601 format).
*   **updatedAt** (string): Timestamp when the invoice was last updated (ISO 8601 format).
*   **totalSummary** (object): Summary of the invoice totals.
    *   **subTotal** (number): Subtotal amount before discounts.
    *   **discount** (number): Total discount amount.

### 7. OPTIONAL PARAMETERS
Not specified in documentation. All fields provided in the schema are present in the example payload, implying they are consistently sent as part of the InvoiceSent webhook event.
