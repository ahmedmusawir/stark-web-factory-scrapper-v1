These are the fields that will be present in the JSON body of the `OpportunityStatusUpdate` webhook event.

*   `locationId` (string): The unique identifier of the location (sub-account) where the opportunity was updated.
*   `id` (string): The unique identifier of the opportunity that was updated.
*   `assignedTo` (string): The unique identifier of the user assigned to the opportunity.
*   `contactId` (string): The unique identifier of the contact associated with the opportunity.
*   `monetaryValue` (number): The monetary value of the opportunity.
*   `name` (string): The name of the opportunity.
*   `pipelineId` (string): The unique identifier of the pipeline the opportunity belongs to.
*   `pipelineStageId` (string): The unique identifier of the pipeline stage the opportunity is currently in.
*   `source` (string): The source from which the opportunity originated.
*   `status` (string): The current status of the opportunity (e.g., "open", "won", "lost").
*   `dateAdded` (string): The date and time when the opportunity was added, in ISO 8601 format.
