### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS (Webhook Payload Fields)
*   **_id (string)**: The unique identifier of the invoice.
*   **status (string)**: The status of the invoice, e.g., "paid".
*   **liveMode (boolean)**: Indicates if the event occurred in live mode.
*   **amountPaid (number)**: The amount paid for the invoice.
*   **altId (string)**: An alternative ID, possibly related to the associated entity (e.g., location ID).
*   **altType (string)**: The type of the alternative ID (e.g., "location").
*   **name (string)**: The name of the invoice.
*   **businessDetails (object)**: Details about the business associated with the invoice.
    *   **name (string)**: Name of the business.
    *   **address (string)**: Address of the business.
    *   **phoneNo (string)**: Phone number of the business.
    *   **website (string)**: Website of the business.
    *   **logoUrl (string)**: URL of the business logo.
    *   **customValues (array of string)**: Custom values associated with the business.
*   **invoiceNumber (string)**: The number of the invoice.
*   **currency (string)**: The currency of the invoice (e.g., "USD").
*   **contactDetails (object)**: Details about the contact associated with the invoice.
    *   **id (string)**: The unique identifier of the contact.
    *   **phoneNo (string)**: Phone number of the contact.
    *   **email (string)**: Email address of the contact.
    *   **customFields (array of string)**: Custom fields associated with the contact.
    *   **name (string)**: Name of the contact.
    *   **address (object)**: Address of the contact.
        *   **countryCode (string)**: Country code.
        *   **addressLine1 (string)**: First line of the address.
        *   **addressLine2 (string)**: Second line of the address.
        *   **city (string)**: City.
        *   **state (string)**: State.
        *   **postalCode (string)**: Postal code.
    *   **additionalEmails (array of object)**: Additional email addresses for the contact.
        *   **email (string)**: An additional email address.
    *   **companyName (string)**: Company name of the contact.
*   **issueDate (string)**: The date the invoice was issued (ISO 8601 format).
*   **dueDate (string)**: The date the invoice is due (ISO 8601 format).
*   **discount (object)**: Discount applied to the invoice.
    *   **type (string)**: Type of discount (e.g., "percentage").
    *   **value (number)**: Value of the discount.
*   **invoiceItems (array of object)**: A list of items included in the invoice.
    *   **taxes (array)**: An array for taxes associated with the item (currently empty in example).
    *   **_id (string)**: Unique identifier for the invoice item.
    **productId (string)**: ID of the product.
    *   **priceId (string)**: ID of the price.
    *   **currency (string)**: Currency of the item.
    *   **name (string)**: Name of the item.
    *   **qty (number)**: Quantity of the item.
    *   **amount (number)**: Amount per item.
*   **total (number)**: The total amount of the invoice.
*   **title (string)**: The title of the invoice.
*   **amountDue (number)**: The remaining amount due on the invoice.
*   **createdAt (string)**: Timestamp when the invoice was created (ISO 8601 format).
*   **updatedAt (string)**: Timestamp when the invoice was last updated (ISO 8601 format).
*   **totalSummary (object)**: Summary of totals.
    *   **subTotal (number)**: The subtotal before discounts.
    *   **discount (number)**: The total discount amount.
