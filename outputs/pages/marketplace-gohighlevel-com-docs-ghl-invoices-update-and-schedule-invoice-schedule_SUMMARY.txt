Here's a structured summary of the API documentation for "Update scheduled recurring invoice":

---

### 1. ENDPOINT NAME:
Update scheduled recurring invoice

### 2. CATEGORY:
Invoice (Schedule)

### 3. HTTP METHOD:
POST

### 4. ENDPOINT URL:
`/invoices/schedule/:scheduleId/updateAndSchedule`

### 5. FULL URL:
`https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/invoices/schedule/:scheduleId/updateAndSchedule`

### 6. REQUIRED PARAMETERS:

*   **Header Parameters:**
    *   `Version` (string): API Version. Possible values: [`2021-07-28`]

*   **Path Parameters:**
    *   `scheduleId` (string): Schedule Id. Example: `6578278e879ad2646715ba9c`

*   **Request Body Parameters (Inferred from successful response schema, as no explicit request body schema is provided):**
    *   `name` (string): Name of the invoice.
    *   `schedule` (object): Details about the recurring schedule.
        *   `rrule` (object): Recurrence rule.
            *   `intervalType` (string): Type of interval for recurrence. Possible values: [`yearly`, `monthly`, `weekly`, `daily`, `hourly`, `minutely`, `secondly`]
            *   `interval` (number): The interval value.
            *   `startDate` (string): Start date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
    *   `invoices` (array of objects): List of invoice details.
        *   Each object in `invoices` array:
            *   `_id` (string): Invoice Id.
            *   `businessDetails` (object): Business Details.
            *   `currency` (string): Currency for the invoice.
            *   `contactDetails` (object): Contact Details.
                *   `id` (string): Contact ID.
                *   `name` (string): Contact Name.
                *   `phoneNo` (string): Contact Phone Number.
                *   `email` (string): Contact Email.
            *   `issueDate` (string): Issue date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
            *   `dueDate` (string): Due date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
            *   `invoiceItems` (array of objects): List of items in the invoice.
                *   Each item object: `taxes` (array), `_id` (string), `productId` (string), `priceId` (string), `currency` (string), `name` (string), `qty` (number), `amount` (number).
            *   `total` (number): Total Amount.
            *   `title` (string): Title of the invoice.
            *   `amountDue` (number): Total Amount Due.
    *   `businessDetails` (object): Business Details for the schedule template.
        *   `name` (string): Business Name.
        *   `address` (object): Business Address.
            *   `addressLine1` (string)
            *   `city` (string)
            *   `state` (string)
            *   `countryCode` (string)
            *   `postalCode` (string)
    *   `currency` (string): Currency for the schedule template.
    *   `contactDetails` (object): Contact Details for the schedule template.
        *   `id` (string): Contact ID.
        *   `name` (string): Contact Name.
        *   `phoneNo` (string): Contact Phone Number.
        *   `email` (string): Contact Email.
    *   `items` (array of objects): Invoice Items for the schedule template.
        *   Each item object: `taxes` (array), `_id` (string), `productId` (string), `priceId` (string), `currency` (string), `name` (string), `qty` (number), `amount` (number).
    *   `total` (number): Total Amount for the schedule template.
    *   `title` (string): Title for the schedule template.
    *   `termsNotes` (string): Terms notes for the schedule template.

### 9. RESPONSE FORMAT:

*   **200 OK - Successful response:**
    *   **Content Type:** `application/json`
    *   **Schema:** Returns a comprehensive JSON object representing the updated invoice schedule. Key fields include:
        *   `_id` (string): Schedule Id.
        *   `status` (string): Schedule Status (e.g., `draft`).
        *   `liveMode` (boolean): Indicates live mode.
        *   `altId` (string): Location or Agency Id.
        *   `altType` (string): Type of altId (e.g., `location`).
        *   `name` (string): Name of the invoice.
        *   `schedule` (object): Contains recurrence rule details (`rrule`, `executeAt`).
        *   `invoices` (array of objects): A list of invoice instances associated with the schedule, each with its own `_id`, `status`, `amountPaid`, `businessDetails`, `contactDetails`, `issueDate`, `dueDate`, `invoiceItems`, `total`, `amountDue`, etc.
        *   `businessDetails` (object): Details of the business.
        *   `currency` (string): Currency used.
        *   `contactDetails` (object): Details of the contact.
        *   `discount` (object): Discount details.
        *   `items` (array of objects): Invoice items template.
        *   `total` (number): Total amount of the schedule template.
        *   `title` (string): Title of the schedule template.
        *   `termsNotes` (string): Terms notes.
        *   `compiledTermsNotes` (string): Compiled terms notes.
        *   `createdAt` (string): Timestamp of creation.
        *   `updatedAt` (string): Timestamp of last update.
*   **400 Bad Request:**
    *   **Content Type:** `application/json`
    *   **Schema:** `{ "statusCode": 400, "message": "Bad Request" }`
*   **401 Unauthorized:**
    *   **Content Type:** `application/json`

This API endpoint is used to update an existing scheduled recurring invoice, allowing modifications to its details and recurrence rules.
