# How to Regain Consistency with Facebook Ads After the Andromeda Update

The Andromeda update shook things up. Here’s the step-by-step framework to bring structure—and results—back to your ad campaigns.

## **The Three-Part System That Works Now**

Think of your ad strategy as a funnel with three campaigns. Each one has its role:

  * **Prospecting – Find the Winners** : Launch a CBO campaign with broad targeting, start lean (~$30/day), test 5–8 creatives weekly, and promote winners.
  * **Remarketing – Build Trust** : Target warm audiences, use testimonials and FAQs, dedicate ~20% of daily spend.
  * **Scaling – Maximize Return** : Move proven ads into a scaling campaign, start with $100–$200/day, and raise budgets 5–10% gradually.

## **Build a Playbook That Wins**

The Andromeda update made one thing clear: your creative does the targeting.

  * **Research First** : Define who your customer is, what problem they need solved, and why your offer is unique.
  * **Creative Angles** : Pain points, desires, objections, and promises.
  * **Formats** : Rotate static images, UGC, videos, and before/after shots.
  * **Copy Framework** : Hook → Pain → Relief → Process → Proof → CTA.



Tip: Refresh creatives weekly. Creative fatigue kills performance faster than poor targeting.

## **Scale Without Losing Control**

Manual bidding helps you stay profitable while scaling:

  * Set bid caps just below your break-even CPA.
  * Raise bids slowly in small increments.
  * Ensure budgets are about 20× your target CPA.

## **Your New Weekly Routine**

Consistency comes from repetition. Here’s your rhythm:

  1. Test broadly in Prospecting.
  2. Move winners into Scaling.
  3. Retarget warm audiences.
  4. Refresh creatives every week.
  5. Track results in a playbook.
